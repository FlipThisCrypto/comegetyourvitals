# COME GET YOUR VITALS

\[Intro\] Yeah. Intake's here. Of course intake's here. Somebody find
the pulse ox.

\[Verse 1\] Come get your vitals, baby, prove you're still alive, Whole
floor negotiating like it's medication night. "Provider said he changed
it, said that I could have..." That's crazy, 'cause I talked to him. He
ain't say none of that.

Chest tight, two days, ain't took a shit, Still somehow got the strength
to argue doses at the desk. "I don't wanna miss group." Aw, look at you
committed, Funny how group gets important when the med line gets
specific.

Pulse ox missing, naturally, Everybody in the building got a nursing
faculty. They think they nurses, diagnosing from the hall, WebMD with a
wristband, now they know it all.

\[Pre-Chorus\] I got one hand on the MAR, one eye on the door, One ear
on the radio asking what now for? Then that little box screams and kills
my peace: INTAKE'S HERE. ...Of course it is.

\[Chorus\] Come get your vitals, Let me see what you're made of. Blood
pressure high, but that ain't what'll save ya. I can take your pulse,
can't make you choose life, Can't prescribe willingness, can't dose you
right. Come get your vitals, Yeah, fuck around, find out, We keep a Code
Blue tucked behind a smart mouth. You came here half-dead saying, "Maybe
I will..." Then one day you look me dead in the eyes: "I CAN AND I
WILL." That's vital.

\[Verse 2\] Valium, muscle relaxers, everybody got a favorite, Pain
walks in demanding that somebody sedate it. But pain got double vision,
it'll bargain, it'll bluff, Sometimes what you're asking for ain't
what'll fix you up. "The crops were dusted." Well, there goes the night.
Somebody faking seizures like we can't tell right. And somebody's mad a
bra became today's catastrophe, Meanwhile I'm trying to keep twenty
people medically... Alive. That's the punchline nobody sees, We joke
dark because the dark works twelve-hour shifts with me. Chaos ain't a
problem here, chaos clocks in, Pulls up a chair at the nurses' station
like, "Y'all hiring?"

\[Chorus\] Come get your vitals... "I CAN AND I WILL." That's vital.

\[Bridge\] See, the hardest one to treat ain't the one screaming. It's
knowing when somebody just ain't ready yet. Knowing addiction don't
check résumés, bank accounts, last names, good homes, bad homes, clean
clothes, track marks. It can happen to anybody. So we take another
pressure. Take another intake. Take another "Where's the provider?" Take
another bad day. Because sometimes... they come back. Not for Valium.
Not for a muscle relaxer. Not because their chest is tight. They come
back just to say: "Thank you. I'm still clean."

\[Final Verse\] That's the high we don't dispense from a cart, Watching
somebody get their name back piece by part. From "I can't" in admission
to "I can" when they leave, Funny thing about a pulse, it ain't the same
as being free. We measure respiration, but you gotta choose to breathe,
We can stabilize your body, can't make your spirit believe. So when
somebody makes it and keeps walking after us, That's the only kind of
refill that will ever be enough. Code Blue if they drop. Code truth if
they lie. Code chaos every time another intake arrives. Call nursing,
call the provider, call whoever you please... Just don't call down here
asking where the provider is.

\[Final Chorus\] COME GET YOUR VITALS, Show me you're alive. Numbers on
a screen don't tell me what's inside. I can take your pulse, can't make
you choose life, But I've watched "I can't" turn into "I WILL"
overnight. Come get your vitals, Fuck around, find out, We laugh in the
fire while we're putting fires out. Addiction takes anybody, Recovery
can too. CODE BLUE! ...and whatever you do: DON'T. BE. A. MELODY.

\[Outro\] Intake's here. ... Goddammit.
