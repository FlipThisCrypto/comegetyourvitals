# MUSIC STYLE REFERENCE

Aggressive female rap, fast-paced modern hip-hop, hard-hitting distorted
808s, huge punchy kick, crisp trap hi-hats, dark bass, sparse ominous
piano and medical-monitor textures. Confident gritty female lead with
sharp articulation, rapid technical flows, sarcastic delivery, dark
humor, clever punchlines, internal rhymes and sudden cadence changes.

Verses should feel controlled and dangerous rather than shouted. Chorus
becomes massive and chantable with layered female gang vocals on "COME
GET YOUR VITALS" and "I CAN AND I WILL." Brief dramatic beat drop during
the emotional bridge with intimate spoken delivery, then an explosive
bass-heavy beat return for the final verse.

Blend nursing-station chaos, gallows humor, compassion and recovery
without becoming sentimental. Occasional radio chatter, monitor beeps
and distant intercom textures used rhythmically. Modern polished
production, cinematic low end, approximately 150 BPM, swaggering,
confrontational, funny, emotionally surprising.
