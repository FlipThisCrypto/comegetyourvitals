# CREATIVE BIBLE

## Story

The fishbowl is both literal and metaphorical. Nursing works in the
center while everyone can see them and everyone wants something from
them. Detox represents the first, murky stage of recovery. Residential
represents stabilization, color returning, and a growing ability to
participate in life. The same water connects both sides.

## Visual arc

### Opening

Begin outside the glass. The station feels like a real aquarium embedded
in a treatment facility. Push through the glass as the first vocal
begins. The audience realizes the nurses live inside the fishbowl.

### Verse 1: controlled chaos

Fast character introductions. Vitals become a visual motif: pulse
waveform, cuff inflation, bubbles pulsing to rhythm. Medication
negotiation can be staged as fish gathering at the med window while a
nurse checks a real coded MAR UI.

### Pre-chorus: intake alarm

Build visual pressure. Radio vibrates. Papers flutter. Puffer starts to
inflate. Everything pauses for a fraction before "INTAKE'S HERE," then
the environment surges.

### Chorus: anthem

Wide ensemble choreography through the central station. Camera orbits
characters while the glass, bubbles and lights remain active. "I CAN AND
I WILL" should be the first genuinely uplifting visual burst, with color
from residential bleeding farther toward center.

### Verse 2: gallows humor

More rapid visual gags: medication requests, pulse ox search, faux
dramatic collapse handled as obvious slapstick, bra joke represented
with a harmless laundry/wardrobe gag, chaos literally clocking in as a
tiny animated timecard/clock motif. Keep it clever rather than mean.

### Bridge: why they do it

Drop the comedy. One recovering fish swims away from the station into
open, clearer water. Nurses watch through the glass. On "It can happen
to anybody," show multiple different silhouettes/species passing through
the same beam of light. On "Thank you. I'm still clean," use restrained
facial acting and a quiet return-to-window moment.

### Final verse/chorus

Color and movement return harder than before. The visual divide is still
present, but the center is brighter. Use a long continuous shot that
travels from detox through the station toward residential while the
nurses perform. "Recovery can too" should visually reverse the opening
darkness.

### Outro

Everything finally settles. One bubble rises. Intercom/radio cue:
"Intake's here." Every nurse freezes, then reacts. Puffer inflates
instantly. Cut to black on "Goddammit."

## Camera language

-   24--35mm equivalent for energetic wide shots
-   50--85mm equivalent for emotional closeups
-   glass crossings for transitions
-   curved aquarium refraction as a motif
-   low-angle tail/fins passing camera for wipes
-   occasional overhead "fishbowl" shot
-   no constant handheld shake
-   detox camera slightly more constrained and claustrophobic
-   residential camera more open and floating

## Palette

Residential: cyan, turquoise, coral, magenta, warm yellow, clean whites.
Center: balanced teal/blue with warm practical highlights. Detox: deep
blue-green, slate, desaturated cyan, cold fluorescent white.

The bridge temporarily reduces saturation everywhere, then warmth
returns.

## Recurring visual jokes

-   pulse ox is always just out of reach
-   puffer inflation corresponds to escalating nonsense
-   "Where's the provider?" causes everyone to look in different
    directions
-   medication line resembles fish gathering at feeding time, without
    dehumanizing clients
-   radio/intercom interrupts at the worst possible moment
-   orca nurse has a deadpan "I have seen everything" reaction
